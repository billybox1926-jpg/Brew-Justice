## Your words matter more than our scales

Use any words you like. There are no right answers.

1. **In your own words: what was it like to play?**

2. **Was there a moment where the game asked too much of you at once?**
   What was on screen just before that?

3. **Was there a moment where the game helped you settle or focus?**
   What led up to it?

4. **Anything you wanted to do that the game made hard to figure out?**

5. **How did the sound feel?** (too much, too little, helpful, grating,
   fine — whatever is true)

6. **How did the visuals feel?** (motion, color, contrast, screen shake,
   flashing)

7. **If you used the settings/options:** did they help? Was anything missing?

8. **Would you keep playing? Why or why not?**
