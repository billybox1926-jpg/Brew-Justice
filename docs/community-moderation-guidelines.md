# Community Moderation Guidelines

These guidelines apply to GitHub Discussions, issue comments, Discord, and any
other community space associated with Brew-Justice. Our community centers
sensory justice and neurodivergent player feedback; keeping that space safe is
part of the project's work, not overhead.

## Values

- **Lived experience is expertise.** Players describing how a feature affects
  them are reporting data, not complaining.
- **Access needs are not preferences.** Requests for reduced motion, audio
  adjustments, pacing controls, or text alternatives are legitimate.
- **Assume good faith, verify impact.** Intent doesn't erase harm; address
  impact first, context second.

## Acceptable feedback tone

Feedback is welcome when it:

- Describes personal experience ("the vignette pulse makes me nauseous")
- Points at specific features, scenes, or settings
- Suggests changes, even tentatively

Feedback crosses a line when it:

- Attacks or diagnoses other participants
- Dismisses access needs as oversensitivity
- Uses slurs, sexual content, or threats of any kind
- Doxxes anyone or drags in off-platform conflicts

## Moderation steps (escalation ladder)

1. **Nudge** - moderator reminds the participant of these guidelines in-line.
2. **Edit/remove** - offending content removed; author notified privately with
   the reason and which guideline applied.
3. **Temporary mute** - repeated disregard: 7-day mute from discussions.
4. **Ban** - harassment, doxxing, or continued pattern after a mute.

Moderators log every action beyond step 1 (what, which guideline, action
taken) in the private moderation log.

## Handling sensitive feedback

Playtest feedback can include mental-health, sensory, and medical context.

- Never quote identifying details from playtest feedback publicly.
- Feedback marked "private" by a participant stays with maintainers only.
- If a participant discloses crisis-level distress, point them to local
  emergency resources; moderators are not counselors and should say so kindly.
- Storage: playtest responses live in the private maintainers' drive, not in
  public issues, unless the participant explicitly consents.

## Scope of these rules

These expectations cover behavior in project spaces. They are not a general
code of conduct for participants' lives elsewhere.

Maintainers may update these guidelines; substantive changes get a 7-day
comment window before taking effect.
